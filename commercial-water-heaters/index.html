Warning Signs to Watch For

Catching a symptom early is often the difference between a quick part swap and a tank that gives out on the garage floor. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Rust-colored water, leaks at fittings, or moisture under the heater
  • Unsteady delivery temperatures that suggest a failing mixing valve
  • Water temperatures that worry you before a health inspection
  • Loud sediment noise from equipment that runs all day
  • Rising gas or electric bills with no change in business volume
  • A recirculation loop that no longer seems to do its job
  • Hot water running out during your busiest hour
  • Equipment approaching the end of its expected service life

Water Heater Care Tips From Our Technicians

A little routine care goes a long way toward keeping a water heater running well past its expected lifespan. Here are our recommendations:

  • Keep the area around the heaters clear for combustion air and service access
  • Confirm the recirculation loop still delivers hot water quickly to the far fixtures
  • Test thermostatic mixing valves periodically to confirm safe delivery temperatures
  • Inspect anode rods regularly, since heavy use consumes them faster
  • Set a flushing schedule based on usage; busy tanks collect sediment fast
  • Log water temperatures at key fixtures so drift is caught early
  • Budget for replacement as equipment nears the end of its expected life
